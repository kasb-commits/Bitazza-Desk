## How to Withdraw Crypto (Mobile App)

1. Go to **Wallet → Crypto → Withdraw**
2. Select the cryptocurrency and network
3. Enter the wallet address (or select from your saved contacts)
4. Enter the amount
5. Enter your **2FA code**
6. A confirmation email will be sent — click the link to confirm
7. Processing begins after email confirmation

**Processing time:** Typically under **1 hour** after email confirmation.

---

## Required: Destination Tags for Specific Coins

The following coins require a **Destination Tag / Memo** when withdrawing to external wallets. Omitting it may result in the funds not being credited at the receiving end:

| Coin | Tag Required |
|------|-------------|
| XRP (Ripple) | Destination Tag |
| XLM (Stellar) | Memo |
| LUNC (Terra Classic) | Memo |
| LUNA (Terra) | Memo |
| HBAR (Hedera) | Memo |
| EOS | Memo |

Always check whether the destination exchange or wallet requires a tag before withdrawing.

---

## Saving Wallet Address Contacts

To avoid re-entering addresses every time:
1. During withdrawal, tap **Add Contact**
2. Enter a contact name and wallet name
3. Save — the address will appear in your **Contact List** for future withdrawals

---

## Withdrawal Status Explained

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending 2FA / Waiting For Email Confirmation | Not yet processed — confirm via email |
| Submitted | Sent to the blockchain — processing |
| Completed / Fully Processed | Transaction complete on blockchain |
| Failed | Withdrawal failed — funds returned to your balance |

---

## How to Cancel a Crypto Withdrawal

- **Before email confirmation:** Go to Dashboard → find the transaction → click **Cancel** if status shows "Waiting For Email Confirmation"
- **After confirming the email:** The withdrawal **cannot be cancelled** — it has been submitted to the blockchain

---

## Sent to Wrong Address

If you confirmed the withdrawal email and the funds were sent to an incorrect address:
- **Blockchain transactions are irreversible** — Bitazza cannot recover funds sent to the wrong address
- Always double-check the full address before confirming the email
- If the wrong address belongs to another Bitazza user, contact support immediately — internal transfers may be recoverable

---

## Why Is My Balance the Same After Withdrawing?

1. You may not have confirmed the withdrawal via the email link
2. Allow **2–3 minutes** after email confirmation for balance to update
3. If unchanged after **1 hour**, contact support with your transaction details

---

## Withdrawal Fees

Crypto withdrawal fees are paid to the blockchain network (not Bitazza) and fluctuate with network congestion. Always check the current fee in the app before withdrawing. Fees are deducted from the amount sent.

---

## Minimum Withdrawal Amounts

Minimum withdrawal amounts vary by coin and network. Check the current minimums in the app before initiating a withdrawal. Withdrawals below the minimum will be rejected.

---

## Where to Find Your TXID

1. Go to Dashboard or Wallet → Transaction History
2. Select the transaction
3. TXID appears when status is "Submitted" or "Completed"

Include your TXID in all support requests related to withdrawals.
