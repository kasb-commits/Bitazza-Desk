## How to Unlock a Locked Account

If your account is locked (e.g. too many failed login attempts), contact support with:

**A selfie holding your ID** with a **handwritten note** showing:
- Website: **bitazza.co.th**
- Request type: **Unlock Account**
- Today's date

Requirements for the selfie:
- Full head clearly visible
- No details blocked or obscured
- ID must be legible

**Processing time:** Within 24 hours.

**Contact:** support@bitazza.com | +66-2-171-2417 | live chat

## How to Change Your Email Address

Email address changes require identity verification and a fee:

1. Email support@bitazza.com or contact via live chat
2. Submit a **selfie holding your passport** (not Thai ID) with a **handwritten note** containing:
   - Website: **bitazza.co.th**
   - Request type: **Change Email**
   - Old email address
   - New email address
   - Reason for change
   - Today's date
3. Pay the **500 THB fee** to: SCB Account 001-353735-5
4. Include proof of payment with your request

**Processing time:** Within 24 hours of document and payment verification.

## Account Frozen / Unable to Trade

If you see an "Account Frozen" notice or cannot trade, it is likely due to **Bitazza's annual customer information review** (required by Thai SEC/AMLO regulations).

**What to do:**
1. Click **"Update Now"** if prompted in the app or website
2. Check your email for messages from any of these addresses:
   - support@bitazza.com
   - notifications@bitazza.com
   - Team-KYC@bitazza.com
   - kyc-ongoing@bitazza.com
3. Complete the requested information update
4. Verification is completed within **24 hours**

> If you did not receive any email and see no "Update Now" prompt, contact support@bitazza.com to check your account status.

## Security Best Practices

1. **Never share your password** with anyone — Bitazza staff will never ask for it
2. **Enable 2FA** — strongly recommended for all accounts
3. **Use a unique password** — do not reuse passwords from other sites
4. **Verify the website URL** before entering credentials — only use trade.bitazza.co.th or the official app
5. **Avoid suspicious emails or links** — Bitazza will never ask you to click a link to "verify" outside the official site
6. **Use trusted devices only** — avoid logging in on public or shared computers
7. **Keep antivirus software** up to date
8. **Use secure connections** — avoid public Wi-Fi for trading
9. **Monitor your login activity** — check for unfamiliar sessions in Profile & Security
10. **Do not share your 2FA codes** with anyone

### Secure Password Requirements
- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character: @#$%^&/?[;

## Beware of Impersonators

Scammers may impersonate Bitazza through:
- Fake Facebook pages or LINE accounts
- Fake apps on app stores
- Fake websites with similar domain names

**Bitazza will never:**
- Ask you to share your wallet address for "verification"
- Ask you to transfer funds to prove account ownership
- Ask for your password, 2FA code, or private key via social media or messaging apps

**If you encounter an impersonator:**
- Do not share any personal information or transfer any funds
- Report to: support@bitazza.com or +66-2-171-2417
- Report the fake account to the platform (Facebook, LINE, etc.)
