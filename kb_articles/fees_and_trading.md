## Trading Fees

The standard trading fee (without any FDM discount) is **0.25%** per trade.

Users can reduce their trading fee by holding FDM tokens in their Bitazza wallet:

| FDM Held | Discount |
|----------|---------|
| 0 FDM | 0% (pay full 0.25%) |
| 50,000 FDM | 50% off → 0.125% |
| 150,000 FDM | 55% off → 0.1125% |
| 500,000 FDM | 60% off → 0.1% |
| 1,500,000 FDM | 65% off → 0.0875% |
| 3,000,000 FDM | 70% off → 0.075% |

Additionally, staking 5,000 FDM earns an extra **+5% discount** on top of the tier above.

## THB (Fiat) Withdrawal Fees

| Detail | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| Fee | 0.001% of withdrawal amount |
| Minimum fee | 20 THB per transaction |
| Maximum single withdrawal | Over 2,000,000 THB (no cap) |
| Processing | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week |

Example: Withdraw 100,000 THB → fee = 100 THB (0.001% = 1 THB, but minimum is 20 THB, so 20 THB applies only if the 0.001% calculation is less than 20 THB).

## First-Time Withdrawal Rule

For THB or crypto withdrawals, users who have just made their **first ever THB deposit** must wait **at least 24 hours** from the time of successful deposit before withdrawing. This is a security requirement.
