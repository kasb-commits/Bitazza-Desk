## How to Deposit Crypto (Website)

1. Go to **My Wallets** and click **Deposit**
2. Select the cryptocurrency and network
3. Your deposit address and QR code will be displayed
4. Copy the address or scan the QR code from the sending platform
5. Send from your external wallet or exchange

---

## How to Deposit Crypto (Mobile App)

1. Go to **Wallet → Crypto → Deposit**
2. Select the cryptocurrency and network
3. Tap the deposit address to copy it, or use the QR code
4. Send from your external wallet or exchange

**Processing time:** Usually a few minutes; can take up to **1 hour** depending on the network.

---

## Destination Tags — Required for Some Coins

The following coins require a **Destination Tag / Memo** in addition to the wallet address. Without it, your deposit may be permanently lost:

| Coin | Tag Required |
|------|-------------|
| XRP (Ripple) | Destination Tag |
| XLM (Stellar) | Memo |
| LUNC (Terra Classic) | Memo |
| LUNA (Terra) | Memo |
| HBAR (Hedera) | Memo |
| EOS | Memo |

Always copy both the address **and** the Destination Tag/Memo from the Bitazza deposit screen.

---

## Why Is My Wallet Address Changing?

Bitazza rotates deposit addresses for security and privacy reasons. This is normal. Your old addresses still work, but it is best practice to generate a new address for each deposit. All addresses for your account route to your wallet.

---

## Crypto Deposit Not Received (After 1 Hour)

1. Verify the transaction is confirmed on the blockchain — look up your TXID on a blockchain explorer
2. Confirm you selected the correct network (e.g. USDT on TRC20 vs ERC20 — these are different)
3. Confirm you included a Destination Tag if required
4. If the blockchain shows the transaction as completed but your Bitazza balance hasn't updated:
   - Contact support with your **TXID**, the **sending platform name**, and a **screenshot showing the successful send**

---

## Deposited Wrong Coins / Unsupported Asset

If you sent a coin that Bitazza does not support to a Bitazza deposit address:
- Bitazza **cannot guarantee recovery** of unsupported tokens
- There is no automatic credit for unsupported assets
- Contact support@bitazza.com immediately with the TXID — recovery may be possible but is not guaranteed and may involve a fee

---

## Minimum Deposit Amounts

- There is **no minimum deposit** for crypto
- However, deposits below the network's minimum may not be credited
- If your deposit is below the minimum and not credited, contact support — recovery requires a CS ticket and may take **5–10 business days**

---

## Critical: Sending to Wrong Network

**Sending USDT on ERC20 to a TRC20 address (or vice versa) will result in permanent loss of funds.** Always verify:
1. The coin you selected in Bitazza matches what you are sending
2. The **network** selected in Bitazza matches the network you are sending on
3. Any required Destination Tag is included

This cannot be reversed once submitted to the blockchain.

---

## Where to Find Your Transaction ID (TXID)

On the mobile app:
1. Go to **Dashboard** or **Wallet → Transaction History**
2. Select the transaction
3. The TXID appears when the status is **"Submitted"** or **"Completed"**

The TXID is the unique blockchain identifier for your transaction — always include it when contacting support about a deposit.
