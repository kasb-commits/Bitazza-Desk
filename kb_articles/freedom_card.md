## What Is the Freedom Card?

The Freedom Card is a **VISA prepaid card** issued by T2P Co., Ltd., regulated by the **Bank of Thailand**. It is integrated with the Freedom World app and the Bitazza ecosystem.

- Works at **80 million+ VISA merchants** worldwide (online and offline)
- Does **not** support ATM cash withdrawals
- Blocked countries: North Korea, Iran
- Virtual Card and Physical Card share the same wallet balance

---

## How to Top Up

Top up via:
- **freedom.cards** (using Deep Pocket login)
- **Bitazza app** — withdraw THB from your Bitazza account to the card

---

## Earning Rewards (FDS)

- Earn **0.5% FDS (Freedom Shards) rebate** on every card transaction above **500 THB**
- FDS is the in-app reward currency for the Freedom World ecosystem
- FDS can be redeemed for real-world rewards in the Freedom World app

---

## Verification Tiers and Spending Limits

| Verification Type | Method | Top-Up Limit |
|------------------|--------|--------------|
| Non Dip-Chip | ID card + facial recognition (in-app) | THB 500,000 |
| Dip-Chip | ID card + physical Boonterm kiosk verification | Higher limits |

To increase your spending limit beyond the Non Dip-Chip tier, complete **Dip-Chip verification** at a **Boonterm Top-up Machine**. Boonterm locations are available across Bangkok districts (Bang Rak, Sathon, Phra Khanong, Chatuchak, Bang Khae, and others — full list on the website).

## FDM vs. FDS

- **FDM** = core utility and governance token of the Freedom ecosystem; used for trading fee discounts on Bitazza, staking, DAO voting
- **FDS (Freedom Shards)** = in-app reward currency earned through card usage, missions, The Scape game; redeemable for perks and real-world rewards
- The original FDM staking program for Freedom Card has ended. All staked FDM + APY has been returned to wallets.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I use the card abroad?**
Yes — at any VISA-accepting merchant worldwide except North Korea and Iran.

**Q: Do my Virtual and Physical cards share the same balance?**
Yes.

**Q: What if my activation QR code is lost?**
Contact support at 091-019-2222.

**Q: Can I use the Freedom Card with my Bitazza wallet?**
Yes — you can withdraw THB from your Bitazza account directly to the Freedom Card wallet.
