## How to Withdraw THB (Website)

1. Go to **My Wallets** and click **Withdraw**
2. Add or select your bank account
3. Enter the withdrawal amount
4. A confirmation email will be sent — click the link to confirm
5. Processing begins after email confirmation

**Withdrawal fee:** 20 THB minimum (0.001% of amount)

---

## How to Withdraw THB (Mobile App)

1. Go to **Wallet → Fiat → Withdraw**
2. First time: tap **Add Bank Account**, select your bank, enter account number, verify
3. Enter the withdrawal amount (minimum: **100 THB**)
4. Enter your 2FA code
5. Confirm via the link in the email sent to your registered address
6. Processing time: **1–4 hours** after email confirmation

**Fee:** 20 THB per withdrawal

---

## Managing Bank Accounts

**To add a bank account:**
- Go to Withdraw → tap **Add Bank Account**
- Select your bank logo, enter account number, verify

**To delete a bank account:**
- Go to Withdraw → tap **Manage**
- Select the account → tap the prohibition/delete icon → confirm

**Important:** The bank account name must exactly match your Bitazza KYC name. Third-party withdrawals are not permitted.

## Withdrawal Limits

| KYC Level | Daily Withdrawal Limit |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Level 1 | 50,000 THB/day (crypto) |
| Level 2 | 200,000 THB/day (crypto) |
| Level 3 | 5,000,000 THB/day (combined) |
| Level 4 | 50,000,000 THB/day (combined) |
| Level 5 | Unlimited |

- Maximum **2,000,000 THB per transaction**
- Overall daily limit: **5,000,000 THB**
- Withdrawals above 5M THB may require additional processing time (2+ days)

---

## Withdrawal Processing Times

- Standard: **1–4 hours** after email confirmation
- High volume periods may take longer
- Amounts above 5M THB: **2+ business days**
- If not received after **4 hours**, contact support

---

## Why Is My Balance Unchanged After Withdrawing?

1. You may not have confirmed the withdrawal via the email link — check your inbox
2. After email confirmation, allow **2–3 minutes** for processing
3. If unchanged after **1 hour**, contact support with your transaction details

---

## Not Receiving Withdrawal Confirmation Emails

1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify you are checking the correct registered email address
3. Check spam/junk/trash folders
4. Ensure your email inbox is not full (free storage exhausted)
5. If still not received: contact support@bitazza.com

---

## Can I Cancel a Withdrawal?

- **Before email confirmation:** Go to My Wallets → Transaction History → Withdraw tab → click **Cancel**
- **After confirming the email:** The withdrawal **cannot be cancelled** — it will be processed

---

## First-Time Withdrawal Rule

After your first THB deposit, you must wait **24 hours** before making any withdrawal (crypto or fiat). This applies once only at account activation.
