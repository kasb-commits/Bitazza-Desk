# How to Create a Bitazza Account

last_verified: 2026-05-09
source: bitazzahelp.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions
category: onboarding

---

## Eligibility

- Minimum age: **20 years old** (required by Thai law)
- Must not be on any sanctions list (UN, OFAC, Thai AMLO)
- One account per person — accounts are non-transferable
- **US residents** are not accepted
- Nationals from FATF blacklisted countries are not accepted

---

## How to Sign Up (Website)

1. Go to **trade.bitazza.co.th/signup**
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a password meeting the requirements:
   - 8–40 characters
   - At least one uppercase letter
   - At least one lowercase letter
   - At least one number
   - At least one special character (@#$%^&/?[;)
4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox
5. Accept the Terms of Use
6. Your account is created — complete KYC to start trading

---

## How to Sign Up (Mobile App)

1. Download the Bitazza app:
   - **iOS:** App Store (search "Bitazza")
   - **Android:** Google Play (package: com.bitazza.freedom.wallet)
2. Tap **Sign Up**
3. Enter your email address
4. Create a password (same requirements as above)
5. Verify your email address via the confirmation link
6. Activate your account
7. Complete KYC to start trading

---

## After Registration — Next Steps

1. **Complete KYC** — required before you can trade, deposit, or withdraw. Go to Profile → KYC Verification and submit your documents.
2. **Enable 2FA** — strongly recommended. Go to Profile & Security → Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
3. **Add a bank account** — required for THB deposits and withdrawals. Go to Wallet → Fiat → Withdraw → Add Bank Account.

---

## Username and Account Details

- Your **username cannot be changed** after account creation
- Personal information (name, address, email, phone) can be updated via profile settings or by contacting support@bitazza.com
- Email changes require identity verification and a 500 THB fee

---

## Can I Trade Without Completing KYC?

No. KYC verification is mandatory before trading. This is required by the Thai Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). You can register without KYC but cannot buy, sell, deposit, or withdraw until verification is complete.
