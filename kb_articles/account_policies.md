# Account Policies: Eligibility, Security, Restrictions, and Termination

## Account Eligibility

- Minimum age: **20 years old** (Thai law requirement)
- Must not be on any sanctions list (UN, OFAC, Thai AMLO list)
- One account per person — accounts are non-transferable
- Must complete KYC to access full trading, deposit, and withdrawal features
- Enterprise/corporate accounts require a nominated Authorized Person

---

## Account Creation

To create an account:
1. Complete the online registration form
2. Provide and verify your email address
3. Pass KYC identity verification (required for trading)
4. Accept the Terms of Use

Your Username cannot be changed after creation. Personal information (name, address, email, phone) can be updated via profile settings or by contacting support@bitazza.com.

---

## Security Features

- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Optional but strongly encouraged. Bitazza may not require it but recommends all users enable it.
- **Session management:** Bitazza may restrict login if: wrong password entered repeatedly, account suspected of hacking, fraudulent or illegal activity detected.
- **Responsibility:** You are responsible for all activity on your account, whether authorized or not. If you suspect unauthorized access, contact Bitazza immediately.

---

## Why Accounts Get Restricted

Bitazza may limit or suspend access in these situations:
1. User violates the Terms of Use
2. Suspicious activity consistent with money laundering, fraud, or terrorism financing
3. Name mismatch between account and deposit/withdrawal bank account
4. First withdrawal after account creation is excessively large
5. Ongoing AML/KYC compliance review required by Thai law (AMLO/SEC)
6. Legal or regulatory requirement
7. User's representations or warranties become false

**Account-specific restriction reason:** The bot cannot tell you why your specific account was restricted. This requires a human agent to access the compliance system. Contact support@bitazza.com.

---

## Account Restriction vs. Account Freeze

- **Restriction:** Certain functions limited (e.g. withdrawals on hold). You can still log in and view balances.
- **Freeze (temporary):** Users can request a voluntary account freeze through profile settings for security reasons (e.g. if you suspect your account was compromised).
- **Suspension by Bitazza:** Full access removed; no trading or withdrawals until resolved.

---

## How to Appeal a Restriction

1. Contact support@bitazza.com with your account email and a description of the issue
2. Bitazza will request any required documentation (source of funds, ID verification, etc.)
3. Proactively submit requested documents — do not wait for a second request
4. Do **not** open multiple tickets for the same issue (it slows the review queue)
5. Review timeline: routine compliance checks take 3–7 business days; AML/enhanced reviews up to 30 business days

Users may also raise a formal objection through the procedure described in the app/website.

---

## Withdrawal Rules

- THB withdrawal methods: PromptPay (registered to your Thai national ID) or direct bank transfer
- Withdrawal must go to a bank account **in your own name** — third-party withdrawals are not permitted
- Name on bank account must match KYC name exactly
- First-time withdrawal requires 24-hour wait after first THB deposit
- Maximum 24-hour processing time for any fiat withdrawal (usually much faster)

---

## Account Closure

Users may terminate their account by:
- Using the account management menu on the site, or
- Contacting the customer center

7 days notice is required. Upon termination: open orders are cancelled, and you have 90 days to withdraw remaining funds before Bitazza may deactivate the account.
