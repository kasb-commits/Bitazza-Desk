## KYC Is Mandatory

KYC verification is required before you can trade on Bitazza. This is mandated by the Thai Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). You can register an account without KYC, but you cannot buy, sell, deposit, or withdraw until verification is complete.

---

## Documents Required by KYC Level

### Level 1 — Thai Nationals
- Thai National ID card — front and back photo
- Selfie holding the ID card
- General personal information (name, address, date of birth)
- Financial information (source of income)
- Compliance disclosure forms
- Suitability test (investment knowledge)
- FATCA questionnaire (US person declaration)
- Crypto risk awareness knowledge test

### Level 1 — Foreign Nationals
- Passport (front page)
- One additional supporting document

### Level 2
All Level 1 documents plus:
- **Proof of Funds** — bank statement or evidence of fund source

### Level 3
All Level 2 documents plus:
- **Proof of Address** — utility bill or bank statement issued within 3 months, showing your name and address
- **Transaction monitoring** — ongoing review applied automatically

### Level 4
All Level 3 documents plus:
- **Proof of Address** (if not already provided at Level 3)

### Level 5
All Level 4 documents plus:
- **Interview with the Bitazza KYC team** — video or in-person

---

## KYC Processing Time

- **Level 1–2:** Guaranteed within **24 hours** during business hours
- Higher levels may take longer depending on document complexity
- Processing outside business hours or during high-volume periods may cause delays

---

## Annual KYC Review (Account Frozen)

Bitazza conducts annual customer information reviews as required by Thai law (AMLO/SEC). If your account is frozen due to an annual review:
- Check for emails from: support@bitazza.com, notifications@bitazza.com, Team-KYC@bitazza.com, kyc-ongoing@bitazza.com
- Click **"Update Now"** in the app or website if prompted
- Verification completes within **24 hours**

## Corporate Account KYC

To open a corporate account:
1. Email support@bitazza.com with:
   - Full name in Thai and English
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Company name
2. Bitazza will respond within **2–3 business days** with the required corporate documentation checklist

---

## Can I Register Without a Thai Bank Account?

Yes — you can register and trade without a Thai bank account. However:
- **Fiat (THB) deposits and withdrawals require a Thai bank account** in your name
- Foreign bank accounts are not supported for fiat transactions
- Crypto deposits and withdrawals do not require a Thai bank account

---

## Country Restrictions for KYC

- **US residents** are not accepted
- Nationals from **FATF blacklisted countries** are not accepted
- All other nationalities are accepted for crypto-only accounts (no THB fiat access without a Thai bank account)

## KYC Rejected — Common Reasons and Fixes

| Rejection Reason | Fix |
|-----------------|-----|
| Blurry or unclear photo | Retake in good lighting, ensure all text is legible |
| Expired ID | Use a valid, non-expired document |
| Name mismatch | Ensure name on ID matches account registration exactly |
| Selfie does not match ID | Take a clear, well-lit selfie holding the ID next to your face |
| Incomplete form | Ensure all fields and tests are completed |

You will receive a notification with the rejection reason. Resubmit with corrected documents.
