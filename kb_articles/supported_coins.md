# Supported Cryptocurrencies on Bitazza

last_verified: 2026-05-07
source: campaign.bitazza.com/crypto/fees
category: trading

---

Bitazza supports **86 cryptocurrencies** for buying, selling, depositing, and withdrawing. All deposits are free of charge.

> **Note:** The definitive and up-to-date list is always in the Bitazza app under Markets. Some coins may have temporary deposit or withdrawal suspensions. Check the app before transacting.

> **Delisted tokens (as of March–April 2026):** SPACE ID (ID), Memecoin (MEME), Metis (METIS), Huma Finance, Jupiter, Berachain, Render, Chiliz (CHZ), Balancer (BAL), Bancor (BNT), Plume, Worldcoin (WLD) — these were delisted in April 2026 with a custody fee of 0.25%/month (minimum 300 THB) applied after delisting.

## Key Warnings

- **Wrong network = lost funds:** Always verify you are sending to the correct network (e.g. USDT on TRC20 to a TRC20 address, not ERC20). This cannot be reversed.
- **Minimum deposit amounts:** Each coin has a minimum deposit. Deposits below the minimum are not automatically credited. Recovery requires a CS ticket and may take 5–10 business days.
- **Network fees:** Crypto withdrawal fees are paid to the blockchain network, not Bitazza. They fluctuate with network congestion. Always check the current fee in-app before withdrawing.
