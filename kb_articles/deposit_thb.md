# THB (Fiat) Deposits: How to Deposit, Issues, and Cancellation

last_verified: 2026-05-09
source: bitazzahelp.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions
category: deposit_issue

---

## How to Deposit THB (Website)

1. Log in and click **Deposit** in the My Wallets tab
2. Enter the THB amount you want to deposit (maximum 2,000,000 THB per transaction)
3. A QR code will be generated — scan it with your mobile banking app, or save and upload it
4. Complete the transfer in your banking app
5. Your balance should update within a few minutes

---

## How to Deposit THB (Mobile App)

1. Go to **Wallet → Fiat → Deposit**
2. Enter the deposit amount
3. Save the QR code or scan it directly from your mobile banking app
4. Complete the transfer

**Supported:** Mobile banking apps (PromptPay QR)
**Not supported:** True Money Wallet, Paotang, or other e-wallets

---

## Important Deposit Rules

- The **name on your bank account must match** your Bitazza KYC name exactly
- Deposits from accounts in a different name will not be credited and require a refund process
- No daily maximum for deposits
- Deposits must come from a **Thai bank account** — foreign bank accounts are not supported for fiat
- Supported deposit method: **PromptPay QR code only** — do not attempt direct bank transfers without a QR code

---

## Deposit Processing Time

- Most deposits are **credited within minutes**
- Allow up to **1 hour** for processing before contacting support
- Deposits outside banking hours may take longer

---

## Deposit Not Received (After 1 Hour)

If your deposit has not appeared after 1 hour:
1. Verify the transfer was successful in your banking app (look for a "success" confirmation)
2. Check that the amount transferred matches what you entered in the Bitazza deposit screen
3. Contact support with:
   - Your registered email
   - Screenshot of the successful transfer from your banking app
   - Amount and time of transfer

---

## Deposit from a Different Bank Account (Name Mismatch)

If you deposited from an account that does not match your Bitazza KYC name:
- The deposit will not be credited automatically
- Contact support via live chat or support@bitazza.com for a refund
- You will need to provide: deposit slip, account name, bank passbook page
- **Refund fee: 100 THB**
- **Refund processing time: 3–7 business days**

---

## How to Cancel a Pending Deposit (Mobile App)

If you generated a deposit QR but have not yet transferred:
1. Go back to the deposit QR page in the app
2. Scroll down and tap **Cancel Deposit**
3. Confirm the cancellation

Once you have transferred funds from your bank, the deposit cannot be cancelled.
