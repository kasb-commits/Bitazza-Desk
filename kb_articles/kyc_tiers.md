## Overview

Bitazza uses a 5-level KYC system regulated by the Thai Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and Anti-Money Laundering Office (AMLO). Higher levels unlock larger daily withdrawal limits.

**Minimum age:** 20 years old (required by Thai law)

**Deposit limits:** All levels have **unlimited daily deposits**.

---

## KYC Level Table

| Level | Daily Deposit Limit | Daily Withdrawal Limit | Additional Requirements |
|-------|--------------------|-----------------------|------------------------|
| Level 1 | Unlimited | **50,000 THB/day** | ID Upload, Selfie, General Info, Financial Info, Other Disclosure, Suitability Test, FATCA Questionnaire, Knowledge Test |
| Level 2 | Unlimited | **200,000 THB/day** | All Level 1 requirements + Proof of Funds |
| Level 3 | Unlimited | **5,000,000 THB/day** | All Level 2 requirements + Transaction Monitoring |
| Level 4 | Unlimited | **50,000,000 THB/day** | All Level 3 requirements + Proof of Address |
| Level 5 | Unlimited | **Unlimited** | All Level 4 requirements + Interview with KYC Team |

> Note: The daily withdrawal limit applies to both THB and cryptocurrency withdrawals combined.

---

## Documents Required by Level

### Level 1 (Basic Verification)
- Thai National ID card (front photo) — บัตรประชาชน
- Selfie holding the ID card
- General personal information (name, address, date of birth)
- Financial information (income source)
- Other compliance disclosure forms
- Suitability test (investment knowledge assessment)
- FATCA questionnaire (for US person declaration)
- Knowledge test (crypto risk awareness)

### Level 2
All Level 1 documents plus:
- **Proof of Funds** — bank statement or other evidence of fund source

### Level 3
All Level 2 documents plus:
- **Transaction monitoring** — ongoing review of trading patterns

### Level 4
All Level 3 documents plus:
- **Proof of Address** — utility bill or bank statement showing current address (typically ≤ 3 months old)

### Level 5
All Level 4 documents plus:
- **Interview with KYC team** — video or in-person interview

## Important Notes

- **Why KYC is required:** Thai law (PDPA + AML obligations under AMLO) mandates identity verification for all digital asset exchange users.
- **First withdrawal delay:** After a first THB deposit, users must wait 24 hours before making any withdrawal (crypto or THB). This applies once only, at account activation.
- **Processing time:** KYC review is typically completed within 1–3 business days for Levels 1–2. Higher levels may take longer.
- **If your KYC is rejected:** You will receive a notification with the reason. Common reasons: blurry photo, expired ID, name mismatch, selfie does not match ID. Resubmit with corrected documents.
- **Account-specific KYC status:** The bot cannot check your individual KYC status. Contact support at support@bitazza.com if you need to check your current level or why a submission was rejected.
