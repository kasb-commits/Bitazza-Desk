## Platform Type: Broker, Not Exchange

Bitazza operates as a **Digital Asset Broker** (not a pure exchange). This means:
- Orders are routed to external liquidity pools and source exchanges
- Bitazza matches prices from multiple global sources to give the best execution
- Local Thai baht (THB) pricing — no need to worry about FX rates for THB users
- Prices shown are final — no hidden spread or additional costs ("what you see is what you get")
- Business hours (8AM–4PM weekdays ICT) typically have the best FX conversion rates for THB

## FDM Token

FDM (Freedom Token) is Bitazza's native utility token. It:
- Gives trading fee discounts (up to 70% off the 0.25% standard fee)
- Powers the broader Freedom ecosystem (Freedom Card, Freedom World app)
- Can be staked for additional +5% fee discount
- Total supply: 2,250,000,000 FDM
