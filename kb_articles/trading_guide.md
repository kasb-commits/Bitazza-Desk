## KYC Required to Trade

KYC verification is **mandatory** before you can trade. You cannot buy or sell any cryptocurrency on Bitazza without completing at least Level 1 KYC. This is required by the Thai Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

---

## Minimum Trading Size

**350 THB** per trade, for all trading pairs. Orders below 350 THB will not be accepted.

---

## How to Trade (Website)

1. Click the **Spot** tab in the top navigation
2. Select a trading pair (e.g. BTC/THB) from the market list or search bar
3. Choose Buy or Sell
4. Select your order type (see below)
5. Enter the amount and confirm

---

## How to Buy & Sell (Mobile App)

**Method 1 — Quick Buy/Sell (Dashboard):**
- Tap any coin from the Dashboard for instant market buy/sell

**Method 2 — Trade Tab (Advanced):**
- Go to the Trade tab
- Select a trading pair
- Choose order type: Market, Limit, or Stop

---

## Order Types

| Order Type | How It Works |
|------------|-------------|
| **Market** | Executes immediately at the current best available price |
| **Limit** | Sets a specific price; executes only when market reaches that price |
| **Stop Market** | Triggers a market order when price hits your stop price |
| **Stop Limit** | Triggers a limit order when price hits your stop price |

### Limit Order Options
- **Good Till Cancel (GTC):** Order stays open until filled or manually cancelled
- **Immediate Or Cancel (IOC):** Fills what it can immediately; cancels the rest
- **Fill Or Kill (FOK):** Must fill the entire order immediately or cancel entirely

---

## How to Cancel an Order

**Website:**
1. Go to the Spot tab
2. Scroll down to **Open Orders**
3. Click **Cancel** next to the order

**Mobile App:**
1. Go to the Trade tab
2. Find your order in Open Orders
3. Tap Cancel

> Only **unmatched (open) orders** can be cancelled. Partially or fully matched orders cannot be cancelled.

---

## My Order Is Not Showing / Not Executing

Common reasons:

1. **Insufficient funds:** Minimum 350 THB required — check your available balance
2. **Order fully matched:** Check the **Matched Orders** or **Order History** tab — it may have already executed
3. **Account status issues:**
   - `Account_Frozen` — your account requires a KYC update; check emails from Bitazza
   - `Exceeds_Daily_Limit` — you have reached your KYC level's daily withdrawal limit

---

## Why Didn't My Limit Order Fully Fill?

Limit orders may partially fill when:
- Price moves rapidly past your limit price before the full order can be matched
- Available volume at your price level is less than your order size

Unfilled portions remain open until cancelled (GTC) or are cancelled automatically (IOC/FOK).

---

## Why Was My Market Order Executed at a Different Price?

Market orders execute at the **average price** across available liquidity, not the exact price shown at order placement. This is normal price slippage — it occurs when:
- Large order size exceeds liquidity at one price level
- Market is moving rapidly at time of order

## Transaction History Export (for Tax / Records)

1. Go to the **FDM tab** in the app or website
2. Select **Trade Reports**
3. Choose **Single** (one-time) or **Cyclical** (recurring) report
4. Select your account and report type:
   - **Trade Activity** — individual buy/sell transactions
   - **Transaction** — deposits, withdrawals, fees
   - **Treasury** — asset balances
5. Click **Generate**, then **Refresh** to check when ready
6. Download as **CSV**
