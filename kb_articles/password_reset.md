# Password Reset and PIN Recovery

last_verified: 2026-05-09
source: bitazzahelp.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions
category: password_2fa_reset

---

## Forgot Password (Website)

1. Go to trade.bitazza.co.th and click **"Forgot Password"** on the login screen
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check your inbox for the password reset email — the reset link is **valid for 30 seconds only**
4. Open the link immediately and enter a new password
5. Password requirements: minimum 8 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character (@#$%^&/?[;)

> **Important:** The reset link expires in 30 seconds. If it expires, go back and request a new one.

---

## Forgot Password (Mobile App)

1. On the login screen, tap **"Can't Login"**
2. Select **"I'm an existing user"**
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Check your inbox for the reset link — valid for **30 seconds only**
5. Open immediately and set a new password meeting the requirements above

---

## Change Password While Logged In (Website)

1. Click the account icon (top right)
2. Go to **Profile & Security → Reset Password**
3. A reset email will be sent — open within 30 seconds
4. Enter and confirm your new password

---

## Forgot PIN (Mobile App)

The 6-digit PIN is separate from your password and 2FA code. To reset it:

1. **Uninstall** the Bitazza app
2. **Reinstall** from the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android)
3. Log in again — you will be prompted to set a new PIN

> Note: Uninstalling does not affect your account, funds, or settings.

---

## Password Requirements

- Minimum 8 characters, maximum 40 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character: @#$%^&/?[;

---
